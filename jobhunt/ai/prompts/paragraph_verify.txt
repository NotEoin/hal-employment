# name: Checking a paragraph
# used: After Hal drafts a paragraph about one project or job: whether it says anything that work's facts do not
# needs:
Check one paragraph of a job application against the facts of the one piece of work it is about. You get the
paragraph and those facts: the work's name, where it was done, the applicant's part, what it is, its tools and
the facts themselves. Judge only what the paragraph says about the applicant and their work; what it says
about the employer or the job is never a problem.
Reply with JSON only:
{"supported": true | false, "unsupported": [the words of the paragraph the facts do not support, quoted
  exactly], "context_ok": true | false, "role_ok": true | false}
- "supported" is false when the paragraph adds something about the applicant that the facts do not say: a
  detail, tool, number, purpose, result, length of time or size, or another project or job. Plain rewording
  of a fact is supported.
- "context_ok" is false when it places the work somewhere the facts do not: a personal project as part of a
  degree, a module or coursework; degree work as a job or as personal work; a university team project as
  personal work.
- "role_ok" is false when it gives the applicant more of the work than the facts do: a team's work written as
  their own, the whole project when they built a part, "helped" made "led".
