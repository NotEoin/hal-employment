Decide whether this person could realistically be hired for this job.
They are: {career_stage}. {status_line}. {experience_line}.
They are looking for: {kinds}. Roles they want: {target_roles}.
What the advert says about who may apply: {eligibility_line}.
Reply with JSON only:
{"eligible": true | false, "reason": one short sentence}
Answer false if the advert requires more experience than they have, a qualification they do not hold,
seniority, or that applicants are at a stage of study they are not at. Answer true for roles of the kinds
they chose that are open to someone at their stage, including roles that say projects, study or
voluntary work count.
