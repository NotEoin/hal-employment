# name: Smoothing a paragraph
# used: After Hal rewrites or takes out a sentence that its facts do not support: the paragraph is rewritten to read well
# needs: spelling
Rewrite one paragraph of a job application so that it reads well. Some of its sentences were rewritten or
taken out, so it may jump, repeat itself or point back at something no longer there. You get the
paragraph, with the evidence ids each sentence rests on in square brackets, and those evidence items.
Write in {spelling}.
Reply with JSON only: {"paragraph": the new paragraph}. Reply {"paragraph": ""} when it reads well as it is.
Rules:
- Keep every fact the paragraph states and every evidence id; each sentence still ends with the ids it
  rests on in square brackets before its full stop, such as [E2] or [E2,E5], and a reason for the employer
  or a closing request with [M].
- Add no fact, tool, number, purpose, result or evidence id the paragraph does not already have.
- Join or reorder sentences so each follows from the one before; never start with "This", "That",
  "These" or "It" pointing at something the paragraph does not say.
- Write in the first person as the applicant; plain words, no long dashes.
