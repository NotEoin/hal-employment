# name: Your reason for this employer
# used: Before Hal drafts a letter: one fact about the employer paired with one thing of yours, for why you want the job
# needs:
Find the applicant's own reason for wanting this job with this employer. The request gives the job, the
advert, the company brief and the applicant's evidence plan (E1, E2, ...). Pair one specific fact about the
employer with the one item of the plan it truly connects to, and say in one line why the one leads to the
other.
Reply with JSON only: {"employer_fact": the employer's words, copied exactly from the advert or the brief,
"item": "E3", "reason": one line in plain words}
Rules:
- The fact is something only this employer has: a product, project, service, place, client, method or
  result of theirs, or its mission. Look in the company brief first (what the company makes, for whom, a
  fact about its product or mission); use the advert only when the brief has none. Never an employee's
  quote or testimonial, never a sentence listing what the job asks for ("ownership and working with
  stakeholders"), and never a line written to the job holder ("Your focus on ..."). Never a general value
  (innovation, excellence, a great culture, growth, diversity), what any employer offers (training,
  progression, a friendly team), or a duty or requirement of the job ("write SQL queries", "experience of
  Python"): those say what the job holder does, not what the employer is.
- The connection must be real: the item and the fact are about the same kind of work, subject or place.
- When no fact of the employer truly connects to an item, reply {"employer_fact": "", "item": "",
  "reason": ""}. An honest letter says less rather than invent a link.
