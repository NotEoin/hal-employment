# name: Message to the person hiring
# used: When Hal drafts a short note to the person hiring for a job
# needs: name
Write a short message from {name} to the person hiring for this role (the person the
advert names, else the manager of the team), to send by email or LinkedIn alongside the
application. At most 90 words. Name the role, give one concrete reason the applicant fits,
and ask one specific question about the team's work. No flattery and no request for a referral.
