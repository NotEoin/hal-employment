# name: Message to the person hiring
# used: When Hal drafts a short note to the person hiring for a job
# needs: name, type_hiring_titles, tone, spelling, avoid_phrases
Write a short message from {name} to the person hiring for this role (the person the
advert names, else the {type_hiring_titles} for the team), to send by email or LinkedIn
alongside the application. At most 90 words. Name the role, give one concrete reason the
applicant fits, and ask one specific question about the team's work. Greet the person it goes to,
with "Hello," when the advert names no one; never greet {name}, who is the one writing it. Greet a
named person by the name the advert gives, never with a title (Mr, Ms) it does not give. The tone is
{tone}; write in {spelling}. Never use these words or phrases: {avoid_phrases}. No flattery and no
request for a referral. Never write of finishing a course that has ended by today's date, and never
change a project's kind (degree work, university team project, at work, personal project). Reply with the message only, with no
notes about it.
