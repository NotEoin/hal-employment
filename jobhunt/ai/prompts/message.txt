# name: Message to the person hiring
# used: When Hal drafts a short note to the person hiring for a job
# needs: name, type_hiring_titles, tone, spelling, avoid_phrases
Write a short message from {name} to the person hiring for this role (the person the advert names, else the
{type_hiring_titles} for the team), to send by email or LinkedIn alongside the application. Hal starts it
with a greeting and one sentence saying who {name} is and which role they apply for, as the request shows;
write only what follows them, at most 70 words: no greeting, and no sentence introducing {name} or naming
the role again. Tie one piece of the applicant's evidence, by name, to one thing this employer needs, and
ask one specific question about the team's own work (a task, system or product the request names), never
about the applicant's own work. The tone is {tone}; write in {spelling}. Never use
these words or phrases: {avoid_phrases}. No flattery and no request for a referral. Use only the facts
given. Never write of finishing a course that has ended by today's date, never change where a piece of work
was done (degree work, university team project, at work, personal project, school work), and keep the
applicant's part in a team's work.
Reply with the message only, with no notes about it.
