# name: Message to the person hiring
# used: When Hal drafts a short note to the person hiring for a job
# needs: name, type_hiring_titles, tone, spelling, avoid_phrases
Write a short message from {name} to the person hiring for this role (the person the advert names, else the
{type_hiring_titles} for the team), to send by email or LinkedIn alongside the application. Hal starts it
with a greeting and one sentence saying who {name} is and which role they apply for, as the request shows;
write only what follows them, at most 70 words: no greeting, and no sentence introducing {name} or naming
the role again. Give one concrete reason the applicant fits,
and ask one specific question about the team's work. The tone is {tone}; write in {spelling}. Never use
these words or phrases: {avoid_phrases}. No flattery and no request for a referral. Never write of
finishing a course that has ended by today's date, and never change a project's kind (degree work,
university team project, at work, personal project). When the request gives an evidence plan, state
nothing about the applicant that its items do not say, keep each item's context and the applicant's part,
and end each sentence about the applicant with the ids of the items it rests on, in square brackets before
its full stop ("[E2]" or "[E2,E5]"); end the question about the team's work, and any other sentence, with
[M]. Hal checks and removes these markers. Reply with the message only, with no notes about it.
