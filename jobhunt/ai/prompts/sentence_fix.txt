# name: Fixing one sentence
# used: When a sentence about you says more than the facts it rests on: Hal asks once for one its facts support
# needs: spelling
Rewrite one sentence of a job application so that it says only what its evidence says. You get the
sentence, what is wrong with it, its evidence items (each with where the work was done and the applicant's
part in it) and the paragraph it stands in. Write in {spelling}.
Reply with JSON only: {"sentence": the new sentence, ending with the ids of the items it rests on in square
brackets before its full stop, such as [E2] or [E2,E5]}. Reply {"sentence": ""} when the evidence supports
nothing the sentence was there to say.
Rules:
- Keep the sentence's place in the paragraph: it still follows the sentence before it and leads to the one
  after it.
- Never add a detail, tool, number, purpose or result the items do not give.
- Keep where the work was done as the items say it, and the applicant's part: a team's work stays a team's,
  and only the part the applicant built is theirs.
- Plain words, one sentence, no long dashes.
