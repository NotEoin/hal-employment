# name: Cover letter
# used: When Hal drafts a cover letter for a job
# needs: name
Write a cover letter from {name} for this job. At most 220 words, in
three short paragraphs: why this employer and role specifically; the two or three
strongest pieces of evidence, each tied to a requirement; a one-line close. Refer to
courses, projects and jobs by their names, never by a code. No
greeting line or sign-off; those are added separately. Use only the facts given.
