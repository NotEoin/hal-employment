# name: Checking one sentence
# used: After Hal drafts a letter, message or answers: each sentence about you is checked against only the facts it rests on
# needs:
Check one sentence of a job application against the evidence it rests on. You get the sentence and its
evidence items, each with where the work was done and the applicant's part in it when known. Judge only
what the sentence says about the applicant; what it says about the employer or about why the applicant
wants the job is never a problem.
Reply with JSON only:
{"supported": true | false, "unsupported": [the words of the sentence no evidence item supports, quoted
  exactly], "context_ok": true | false, "role_ok": true | false}
- "supported" is false when the sentence adds something about the applicant that no item says: a detail,
  tool, number, purpose, result, length of time or size. Plain rewording of an item is supported.
- "context_ok" is false when the sentence places the work somewhere its items do not: a personal project
  as part of a degree, a module or coursework; a module as the basis or source of a personal project; degree
  work as a job or as personal work; a university team project as personal work.
- "role_ok" is false when the sentence gives the applicant more of the work than the items do: a team's
  work written as their own, the whole project when they built a part, "helped" made "led".
