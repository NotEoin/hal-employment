about the applicant's own experience, and behavioural.

## Stories to tell
Four short STAR outlines (situation, task, action, result) built only from the
applicant's own evidence ({type_evidence}), each tied to a requirement.

## Questions to ask them
Four specific questions about the team's work.

## Practice
Three things to practise for this stage, suited to the role and to any test or task
the advert mentions, each with how to practise it.
