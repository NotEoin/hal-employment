# name: How you fit the job
# used: When Hal prepares documents for a job: each requirement matched to your evidence
# needs: career_stage, type_evidence
Match a candidate's evidence to a job's requirements. The candidate is {career_stage}.
The evidence that counts most for them is {type_evidence}.
Reply with JSON only:
{"matches": [{"requirement": ..., "evidence": the named piece of evidence (for example their
  {type_evidence}) that shows it, in at most 20 words, "strength": "strong" | "partial"}],
 "gaps": [requirements the CV gives no evidence for],
 "eligible": true | false, "eligibility_notes": why, in one sentence}
Rules: use only the CV text and the employment record, whose passages come from the applicant's own
documents. Cite record evidence as "record: <what it is>".
Never cite the summary or a skills list as evidence,
except for a requirement that names a language or tool. "strong" means the evidence
directly demonstrates the requirement. A requirement with no evidence goes in gaps.
Judge eligibility against the conditions given and the candidate's dates; with no conditions
given, "eligible" is true. Experience from internships, study, volunteering or personal projects counts where the advert allows it;
a preference ("desirable", "ideally", "a plus") is not a condition.
