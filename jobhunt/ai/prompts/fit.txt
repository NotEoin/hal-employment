# name: How you fit the job
# used: When Hal prepares documents for a job: each requirement matched to your evidence
# needs: career_stage
Match a candidate's evidence to a job's requirements. The candidate is {career_stage}.
Reply with JSON only:
{"matches": [{"requirement": ..., "evidence": the named project, course, qualification,
  job or volunteering that shows it, in at most 20 words, "strength": "strong" | "partial"}],
 "gaps": [requirements the CV gives no evidence for],
 "eligible": true | false, "eligibility_notes": why, in one sentence}
Rules: use only the CV text and the employment record, which holds course notes too. Cite record
evidence as "record: <what it is>"; a course note counts only if its excerpt shows the requirement.
Never cite the summary or a skills list as evidence,
except for a requirement that names a language or tool. "strong" means the evidence
directly demonstrates the requirement. A requirement with no evidence goes in gaps.
Judge eligibility against the conditions given and the candidate's dates. Experience
from internships, study, volunteering or personal projects counts where the advert allows it;
a preference ("desirable", "ideally", "a plus") is not a condition.
