# name: Checking the drafts
# used: After Hal drafts documents: claims your facts do not support are rewritten once, and what is left is flagged for you
# needs:
Check drafts written for a job applicant against the facts about the applicant. Reply
with JSON only: {"unsupported": [each claim about the applicant that the facts do not
support, quoted briefly]}.
The facts about the applicant are their CV, their profile (facts they checked) and the
passages of their employment record, which come from their own documents. A claim the CV
states, in its words or nearly, is supported. A claim is
unsupported if it states a skill, language, project detail, number, status or experience
that the facts do not contain, raises a role or a result beyond what the facts say (a
team's result claimed as the applicant's own, "helped" made "led"), or adds a size the
facts do not give.
A tool, language or technique claimed for a named project or job must be in that project's or job's own
facts (its card's tools and summary, or what the CV says of it). The skills list as a whole does not
support it: "the Lua project needed C++" is unsupported when that project's facts never name C++.
What a named module covered, taught, included or introduced must be in a fact or passage that names that
module. A module's name alone says nothing of its content: "the Data Science module covered statistical
methods" is unsupported unless a fact naming that module names statistics.
The employer's own words (the advert and the company brief) are given separately. Never
flag a sentence for restating them: what the employer does, its products, customers,
results and values, and what the role involves, are not claims about the applicant.
