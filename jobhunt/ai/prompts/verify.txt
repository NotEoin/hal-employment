# name: Checking the drafts
# used: After Hal drafts documents: claims the facts do not support are flagged for you
# needs:
Check drafts written for a job applicant against the facts about the applicant. Reply
with JSON only: {"unsupported": [each claim about the applicant that the facts do not
support, quoted briefly]}. A claim is unsupported if it states a skill, language,
project detail, number, status or experience that the facts do not contain, raises a
role or a result beyond what the facts say (a team's result claimed as the applicant's
own, "helped" made "led"), or adds a size the facts do not give.
Claims about the company are out of scope.
