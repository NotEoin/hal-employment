# name: Choosing the evidence
# used: Before Hal drafts a letter, a message or answers: the facts about you the draft may rest on
# needs:
Choose the evidence a job application will rest on. The request gives the job's requirements and
questions, then the applicant's facts as numbered sources (S1, S2, ...): lines of their CV, facts they
checked and passages of their own documents. Pick the strongest pieces of evidence for this job, the
best first, no more than the request allows.
Reply with JSON only:
{"evidence": [{"source": "S3", "quote": the words of that source the evidence rests on, copied exactly,
  "serves": the requirement or question it answers, in a few words}]}
Rules:
- Copy each quote word for word from its one source: never change, shorten inside, join or add words.
  A quote of one clause or sentence is best; never more than two sentences.
- Pick facts about the applicant only, never about the employer.
- Prefer a named project, job, module or result over a general skill; pick each fact once.
- A project's facts include who did the work: when a source says it was a team's or names the
  applicant's part, quote the words that say so.
