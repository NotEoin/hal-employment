# Get job and internship alerts by email

Many UK student and graduate job sites send alerts by email. When Hal reads your email, it turns each job
in an alert into a match, scored against your goal like any other. This is the best way to hear about paid
internships, placement years, insight weeks and graduate schemes, because many are advertised only on these
sites.

Hal reads alerts only from the inbox you set up in Settings, Email. Set that up first.

1. Decide which kinds of role you want: open **Settings**, **What you're looking for**, **Kinds of role**.
   Ask for alerts only for those kinds, so your matches stay useful.
2. Sign up for alerts on the sites that suit you, using the email address Hal reads:
   - Students and new graduates: RateMyPlacement, Bright Network, Prospects, TARGETjobs, Milkround.
   - Engineering, science and technology: Gradcracker.
   - Charities, campaigns and the arts: CharityJob, Guardian Jobs, Arts Jobs, the Museums Association.
   - Politics and policy: W4MP.
   - Government and the public sector: Civil Service Jobs, NHS Jobs.
   - Universities and research: jobs.ac.uk.
   - Apprenticeships: Find an apprenticeship (on GOV.UK).
3. On each site, choose the kinds of role, subjects and places you want, and ask for a daily or weekly email.
4. Wait for the first alert. Hal reads your email every few minutes; the jobs appear in **Jobs**, Matches,
   and **Where Hal looks** counts them on the "Your email job alerts" row.

Indeed and LinkedIn alerts are read too. To read alerts from a site not listed here, add its sender's
address in **Settings**, **Email**, **Job alert senders** (under Show more). That list then replaces Hal's
own, so add every sender you want.

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Your email job alerts" says "Set up email" | Hal is not reading an inbox yet. Open Settings, Email and follow the steps. |
| An alert arrived but no jobs appeared | The alert's jobs may all be hidden by your goal: look in Jobs, Hidden. If they are not there either, add the sender in Job alert senders and say which site it was in "Something wrong here?". |
| Jobs from an alert have the site's name as the employer | The alert did not say who the employer is. Open the job: Hal reads the advert for the first few jobs of each alert. |
| An email about one of your applications was read as an alert | Tell Hal in Messages; replies about applications should reach the application, and Hal keeps those apart by their subject. |
| You get too many alerts | Change the alert on the site itself, or unsubscribe there. Hal never unsubscribes for you. |
