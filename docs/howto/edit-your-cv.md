# Edit your CV line by line

**My CV**, **Edit your CV** shows Hal's copy of your CV as you can change it: each section, then its entries
(a job, a qualification, a project), then each entry's lines. Nothing changes until you press **Save
changes**, and each save is a new version, so you can always go back.

1. Open **My CV** and choose the **Edit your CV** tab.
2. Change what you need:
   - Type in any field or line to change its words.
   - **Add a line** adds a line to an entry. **Add an entry** adds an empty job, qualification or project.
   - **Add from your record** lists the jobs, education, projects and certificates in **Your record** that fit
     the section. **Add** copies one in as it is: a job's title, employer, dates and duties, a qualification
     with its grade and modules, a project's name, link, tools and summary, or a certificate.
   - The up and down arrows move an entry or a line. The bin deletes it.
   - **Show** off keeps an entry on file but leaves it out of the CV you send.
   - The lock keeps an entry or a line as it is: Hal never suggests a change to it.
3. Press **Save changes**. Hal saves everything you changed as one new version and says its number.
4. To undo a save, open **Versions** and press **Use this version** on the version before it.

Lines you typed or reworded are marked **Your words**. Hal treats them as yours: its suggestions never call
them a writing problem to fix. Lines added from your record are marked **From your record**.

## Edit your CV for one job

When Hal writes the documents for a job, the **CV** tab of that application's **Documents** step shows the
CV Hal made for it in the same editor, with the PDF Hal will upload beside it. Your main CV stays as it is.

1. Open the application and its **CV** tab. If **Ask me what the job cares about first** is on (**Settings**,
   **CV and documents**), the tab first asks **What does this job care about most?** and lists the job's
   requirements. Tick three to five of them and press **Adjust my CV for these**. Hal makes the job's CV
   again from what you ticked, then shows it here.
2. Read **What Hal changed for this job**. Each change says what kind it is (**Reworded**, **Moved** or
   **Added**), your CV's words beside the job's, and why. The parts Hal changed are marked **Hal:**
   with the same kind in the CV below.
3. Press **Undo** on any change you do not want. **Redo** puts it back.
4. Change any line as on **My CV**. Parts you changed are marked **Your edit**.
5. Press **Save changes**. Hal keeps your edits for this job and makes the PDF again, so the preview shows
   them.
6. To keep an entry's lines on your main CV too, press **Copy to my main CV** on that entry. Hal copies its
   lines into your main CV as one new version. Nothing else on your main CV changes. When you use several
   CVs for applications, the button names the CV this application was built from, such as **Copy to my
   Museum CV**, and the lines go there.
7. Press **Approve and fill in the form**. Hal saves this job's CV as its own version and uploads that one.

The CV for a job is made from the version of your main CV in use when Hal wrote the documents. A change you
make on **My CV** after that does not reach it: make the change in the job's CV too. If you undo a change of
Hal's on a line you also changed, your words stay.

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Approve and fill in the form** cannot be pressed, and the step says to save or discard your changes | Press **Save changes** or **Discard changes** on the CV tab first. |
| "This job's CV changed since you started editing. Your changes are not saved." | The job's CV was saved from another window meanwhile. Copy what you want to keep, press **Start again from this job's CV now**, and make the changes again. |
| "Your main CV changed since this page opened. Nothing was copied." (with several CVs in use, the CV's name: "Your Museum CV changed ...") | That CV changed after you opened the application. Reload the page, then press the copy button again. |
| "Your main CV already has these lines." (or "Your Museum CV ...") | Nothing to copy: the entry reads the same on both. |
| The CV tab shows only Hal's changes, with no editor | Hal wrote this application's documents before CVs could be edited line by line. Use **Undo** on Hal's changes, or change your main CV on **My CV**. |
| "Save or discard your changes first." after **Undo** or **Copy to my main CV** | Save or discard your edits, then press it again. |
| **Adjust my CV for these** cannot be pressed | Tick three to five of the requirements, not fewer or more. |
| **Approve and fill in the form** cannot be pressed, and the step says to tick what this job cares about most | Tick three to five requirements on the CV tab and press **Adjust my CV for these** first, or turn off **Ask me what the job cares about first** in **Settings**. |

## When something goes wrong on My CV

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Leave without saving?" when you change tab or page | You have changes you have not saved. Press **Stay here**, then **Save changes**, or **Leave without saving** to drop them. |
| "Your CV changed since you started editing. Your changes are not saved." | Your CV was changed somewhere else meanwhile, for example by accepting a suggestion. Copy what you want to keep, press **Start again from your CV now**, and make the changes again. |
| "Your record has nothing to add here yet." | Add the job, qualification, project or certificate on **Your record** first, then press **Add from your record** again. |
| **Save changes** cannot be pressed | Nothing has changed since the last save. |
