# Add a job site, a careers page or a company to watch

Hal searches its own list of UK job sites for you, chosen to suit your career: universities, charities,
the arts, the public sector, business and technology. You can add an employer's careers page, a company
you would like to work for, or a board Hal does not know yet. Hal works out what kind of site it is.

1. Open **Jobs** from the menu on the left, then the **Where Hal looks** tab.
2. Press **Add a job site or careers page** and paste the page's address, for example the "Jobs" or
   "Vacancies" page of an employer's website. Press **Look**.
3. Hal says what it found, for example "This is a Workable careers page for Harbour Arts Trust. Hal can
   check it at every search. It has 4 jobs now.", and lists those jobs scored against your goal. The ones
   worth keeping are ticked. Tick or untick any.
4. Press **Save the site**. The ticked jobs go to Matches, and Hal checks the site at every search from
   now on. Activity says "You added a job site", with **Undo**.
5. To follow a company without knowing its careers page, press **Watch a company** and type its name.
   Hal looks for its job board on every application system it knows (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable,
   SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio). If it finds none, paste the careers page's address instead.
6. To stop searching a site, switch it off. Sites you added can also be removed with the cross.

Hal reads a page by itself when it is on an application system it knows, links a job feed, or carries the
job details search engines read (most large employers' pages do). For any other page, Hal says "Hal cannot
read this page by itself yet. Add one advert instead.": press **Add one advert** on each job you find there.

## Job sites that want a free key

Adzuna and Reed are two large UK job searches. Each needs a free key from its own website. Hal works
without them and says so on the page: "Reed has no key, so Hal skips it." To add one, press **Get a free
key** on its row, follow the two steps, and paste the key.

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Hal cannot read this page by itself yet" | The page has no job details Hal can read. Use **Add one advert** for each job on it, or look for the employer's Workable, Greenhouse or Lever page instead. |
| "Hal cannot read Workday pages by itself yet" | Workday, Teamtailor, BambooHR and Pinpoint pages are not read yet. Add each job with **Add one advert**. |
| "Hal no longer has that look at the site. Press Look again." | Hal was restarted, or the dialog was left open for over half an hour. Press **Look** again. |
| "Hal could not find a job board for ..." | The company uses a system Hal does not know, or a different name. Paste the address of its careers page instead. |
| A site's row says "Last time it said: ..." | The site did not answer at the last search. Press **Search it again**; if it keeps failing, switch it off. |
| "Reed has no key, so Hal skips it." | Nothing is wrong: Hal searches the other sites. Add a free key to search Reed too. |
| A site you removed is back | Only sites you added can be removed. Hal's own sites can be switched off, and stay off. |
