$ jobhunt sources --help
usage: jobhunt sources [-h] [--section SECTION] [--kind KIND] [--card CARD]
                       {list,add,read} [words ...]

positional arguments:
  {list,add,read}
  words              add: the section and a file, folder, link or name; read: its number, or
                     `waiting` for everything still to read

options:
  -h, --help         show this help message and exit
  --section SECTION  list only this section (cv, coursework, project, document, ...)
  --kind KIND        add: what it is within the section (essay, transcript, repo, ...)
  --card CARD        add: the course or job card it is for, by its number
